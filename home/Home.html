Frequently asked questions

Which syllabuses do your tutors support? expand_more

We align our academic and technology tutoring with CBSE, ICSE, and IGCSE curricula across Grades 1 to 12.

Are classes 1-on-1 or in small groups? expand_more

Both options are available. Parents can choose personalised 1-on-1 tutoring or small peer groups (maximum 4 students) matched by grade and pace.

Does my child need prior coding experience? expand_more

Not at all. Beginners start with visual logic or guided Python basics, while students with prior experience jump straight into intermediate projects.

How do parents track progress? expand_more

Tutors share concise monthly progress summaries covering concept mastery, homework consistency, and upcoming focus areas.

How do we book a free consultation? expand_more

Reach out via email, WhatsApp, or our quick Google Form. We’ll schedule a friendly 20-minute chat to understand your child’s goals.
