Questions parents ask before booking

Everything you need to know about our trial sessions, curriculum boards, and how we keep parents informed.

What happens during the free trial class? expand_more

Your child meets a subject specialist for a live, interactive 30-minute mini-lesson tailored to their grade, followed by a 15-minute parent debrief with a personalised learning roadmap.

Which school boards do you support for Maths and Psychology? expand_more

We align directly with CBSE, ICSE, and Cambridge IGCSE syllabi across Grades 6–12, including board exam past-paper practice for Grades 10 and 12.

Can my child combine 1-on-1 Maths with Group Coding? expand_more

Yes. Many families choose 1-on-1 mentoring for exam-heavy subjects like Maths or Grade 11–12 Psychology, and a small-group cohort for project-based Coding.

How do parents track progress each week? expand_more

After every week of classes, parents receive a concise summary covering topics mastered, practice accuracy, and upcoming school-test readiness.
